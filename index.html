Movement library

SEE IT. SET IT. DO IT RIGHT.

Each card includes a visual reference, working sets, rep ranges, rest and one technique cue. Ask a FAT2FIT trainer to check your form before adding weight.

LOWER BODY · BEGINNER

Bodyweight squat

2–3 sets8–12 reps60–90 sec rest

Feet about shoulder-width. Brace your trunk, sit hips down and back, keep knees tracking in line with toes, then stand without bouncing.

Stop if you feel sharp pain or lose balance.
CHEST · SHOULDERS · TRICEPS

Push-up

2–3 sets6–12 reps60–90 sec rest

Keep head, hips and heels aligned. Lower with control, elbows angled roughly 30–60° from your torso, then press away. Use an incline if needed.

Choose a variation that lets you keep full control.
BACK · PULL

Lat pulldown

2–3 sets8–12 reps75–120 sec rest

Secure thighs under the pads, lift chest naturally and pull the bar toward the upper chest without swinging or yanking behind the neck.

Use a manageable load and return the bar slowly.
CHEST · PRESS

Dumbbell bench press

2–3 sets8–12 reps90–120 sec rest

Keep feet planted and shoulder blades stable. Lower dumbbells beside the chest with wrists stacked over elbows, then press up smoothly without crashing weights together.

Use a spotter for challenging loads.
Training rule: These are general adult examples, not a personalised prescription. Start with lighter loads, stop with 2–3 good reps still possible, and increase resistance only when technique stays consistent. Anyone with injury, persistent pain, or a medical concern should seek qualified guidance first.
